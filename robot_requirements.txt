1. The robot must determine the direction to move based on the availability of unblocked paths and the location of its goal.  
2. If the goal is directly ahead and the path in front is unblocked, the robot must move forward.  
3. If the goal is to the left and the path to the left is unblocked, the robot must move left.  
4. If the goal is to the right and the path to the right is unblocked, the robot must move right.  
5. If the goal is not directly in front, left, or right, the robot must still prioritize moving toward the direction where the goal is located, provided the path is unblocked.  
6. The robot must not move into any direction that is blocked.  
7. If all directions are blocked, the robot must stop and remain stationary.  
8. The robot must continuously re-evaluate its path based on the latest input about blocked paths and goal location.  
9. The robot must not assume any information about the environment beyond what is explicitly provided.  
10. The robot must not move unless it is certain that the direction is unblocked and leads toward its goal.  

Allowed robot actions:  
- Move forward  
- Move left  
- Move right  
- Stop
