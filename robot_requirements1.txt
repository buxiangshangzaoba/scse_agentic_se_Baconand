1. The robot must determine the direction of the goal (front, left, or right) relative to its current position.  
2. The robot must prioritize moving toward the goal direction if it is unblocked.  
3. If the goal direction is blocked, the robot must check other directions in the order of front, left, and right to find a safe path.  
4. The robot must never move into a blocked direction.  
5. If no safe directions are available, the robot must stop and remain stationary.  
6. The robot must continuously re-evaluate its surroundings and update its movement decisions based on real-time input.  

Allowed robot actions:  
- Move forward (toward the front direction)  
- Move left (toward the left direction)  
- Move right (toward the right direction)  
- Stop (remain stationary)
